# Methods references

* Chen T., Guestrin C. (2016). *XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System.* KDD.
* Cortes C., Vapnik V. (1995). *Support-vector networks.* Machine Learning 20:273–297. Platt J. (1999), probabilistic outputs for SVMs.
* Loshchilov I., Hutter F. (2019). *Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization* (AdamW). ICLR.
* Ioffe S., Szegedy C. (2015). *Batch Normalization.* ICML. Srivastava N. et al. (2014). *Dropout.* JMLR.
* Bergstra J., Bengio Y. (2012). *Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization.* JMLR 13:281–305.
* Benjamini Y., Hochberg Y. (1995). *Controlling the false discovery rate.* JRSS-B 57:289–300.
* Kruskal W., Wallis W. A. (1952). *Use of ranks in one-criterion variance analysis.* JASA 47:583–621.
* Breusch T. S., Pagan A. R. (1979). *A simple test for heteroscedasticity.* Econometrica 47:1287–1294. MacKinnon J. G., White H. (1985), HC3 robust standard errors.
* Northcutt C. et al. (2021). *Confident Learning: Estimating Uncertainty in Dataset Labels.* JAIR. Inspiration for the out-of-fold label audit.
* Pan J. et al. (2020). *Adversarial Validation Approach to Concept Drift Problem.* Used for the labelled-vs-unlabelled drift check.
* Dempster A. P., Laird N. M., Rubin D. B. (1977). *Maximum likelihood from incomplete data via the EM algorithm.* JRSS-B 39:1–38.
* Hastie T., Tibshirani R. (1996). *Discriminant analysis by Gaussian mixtures.* JRSS-B 58:155–176.
* Nigam K., McCallum A., Thrun S., Mitchell T. (2000). *Text classification from labeled and unlabeled documents using EM.* Machine Learning 39:103–134. Semi-supervised EM as used by the SSGMM.
* Wolpert D. H. (1992). *Stacked generalization.* Neural Networks 5:241–259. XGBoost / SVM on mixture posteriors.
* Little R. J. A., Rubin D. B. (2019). *Statistical Analysis with Missing Data*, 3rd ed. Marginalising Gaussian models over missing inputs.
* Belsley D. A., Kuh E., Welsch R. E. (1980). *Regression Diagnostics.* Collinearity, VIF and orthogonalised predictors.
* Hastie T., Tibshirani R., Friedman J. (2009). *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd ed., §3.2.3 (Gram-Schmidt regression) and §7.10 (one-standard-error rule).
